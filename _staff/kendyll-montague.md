**Kendyll Montague**, a proud UPAC Alumna, developed a passion for dance at an early age attending classes as a UPAC student and member of Troupe. After graduating Montclair High School, Kendyll chose to continue her education at Montclair State University, where she is currently majoring in Early Childhood Education. While continuing her studies, she is a proud member of the Montclair State Co-Ed Cheerleading team, representing MSU during football season, special events and an annual national collegiate competition held in Daytona Beach, Florida. Nurtured by generations of dance majors before her, she excels in dance with a passion for gymnastics and cheerleading contributing to her wide range of skills. Kendyll continues her dance studies to further her skills and extend the best instruction to her students.
