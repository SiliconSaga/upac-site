**Lynsey Duverglas** (UPAC Alumna) is a New Jersey native professional dancer and choreographer who began her dance training at a young age in styles including ballet, modern, jazz, lyrical and hip hop. She recently graduated from the Pennsylvania State University with degrees in Dance and Psychology. While at Penn State she was on the 3x National Champions Lionettes Dance Team, performing at football and basketball games as well as other sporting and community events. Lynsey has been an Apprentice with Liberate Artists Inc. for five years, assisting celebrity choreographers such as Mia Michaels, Lauren Froderman, Eva Igo, Phil Wright and Comfort Fedoke. While obtaining her master's degree at New York University she continues to share her passion for dance with others through teaching and choreography.
