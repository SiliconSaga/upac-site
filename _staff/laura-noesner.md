**Laura Noesner** is thrilled to be teaching here at UPAC. She started dancing at 2 and hasn't stopped since. Although her official college studies were in mathematics, she continued dancing in college by finding a job teaching dance at daycare centers across Northern New Jersey and beyond, and by being highly involved in her college and local community theatre productions. She returned to teaching dance after having her first child, which gave her the opportunity to do something she loved while also spending time with her kids. She is looking forward to helping each dancer she encounters learn and grow in dance and beyond.
