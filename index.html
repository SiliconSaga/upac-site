About UPAC

A place that's different from the typical dance studio

Founded in 1998 by director and owner Lisa C. Batchelor, UPAC is an artistic outlet with a family atmosphere: a place to work toward individual goals while belonging to a cohesive group. Students learn proper technique in ballet, jazz, tap, hip-hop and musical theater, and along the way, teamwork, sportsmanship, responsibility, leadership and confidence.

Every season closes with a Spring Performance on a full-sized stage, with professional sound, lighting, props and backdrops, and a specially choreographed number where dads dance with their kids.

UPAC Troupe

Our award-winning competitive team

A select group of dancers, ages seven to eighteen, chosen for technique, performance quality and dedication. Troupe members train with master teachers, compete regionally and nationally, and perform at community events throughout the year, with the emphasis on self-improvement rather than trophies.
